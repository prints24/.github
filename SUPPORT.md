# Super91 Support Directory

Welcome to the Super91 Support page. Please use the appropriate channel below depending on your request.

---

## 🛍️ Customer, Order & Print Inquiries

If you are a customer inquiring about an active print order, artwork preflight status, delivery tracking, or invoices:

* **Official Website:** [super91.com](https://super91.com)
* **Customer Support Email:** [support@super91.com](mailto:support@super91.com)
* **X / Twitter:** [@super91_](https://twitter.com/super91_)
* **Hours of Operation:** Monday – Saturday, 9:30 AM – 6:30 PM IST

---

## 🏭 Franchise & Production Hub Operational Support

If you are a National Production Hub (NPH), Central Production Hub (CPH), or Delivery Franchise (DF) operator:

* **Hub Dashboard:** Access your operations dashboard on [super91.com/admin](https://super91.com)
* **Logistics & Handover Issues:** Reach out through your designated hub coordinator or franchise manager channel.
* **Barcode & Scanner Issues:** Report hardware/scanning discrepancies via your internal floor manager.

---

## 💻 Developer & Engineering Support

If you are an engineer or contributor working on the Super91 platform:

* **Bug Tracking & Tasks:** Check the [SUPER91 Web V1 - Launch Track](https://github.com/orgs/prints24/projects/2) project board.
* **Issue Filing:** Use the [Structured Issue Templates](https://github.com/prints24/prints24/issues/new/choose) to report bugs or propose enhancements.
* **Technical Inquiries:** Mention `@prints24/leads` or `@prints24/engineering` in relevant issue or pull request discussions.
* **Security Advisories:** Review our [Security Policy](SECURITY.md) for confidential reporting.
