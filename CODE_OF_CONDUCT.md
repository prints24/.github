# Super91 Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community and development organization a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community and workplace.

---

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our team and partners include:

* **Demonstrating empathy and kindness:** Treating teammates, franchise partners, and external contributors with patience and dignity.
* **Constructive collaboration:** Being respectful of differing viewpoints and architectural perspectives.
* **Gracefully accepting constructive feedback:** Fostering a growth mindset where code reviews are treated as collaborative learning opportunities.
* **Focusing on what is best for the platform and users:** Prioritizing reliability, data integrity, and real-world floor usability for our production hubs and delivery franchises.
* **Showing professional integrity:** Respecting internal credentials, client data, and production system access.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
* Public or private harassment.
* Publishing others' private information, such as physical or email addresses, customer contact numbers, or internal credentials, without explicit permission.
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

---

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders and engineering management are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, including GitHub repositories, issue trackers, pull request discussions, and internal communication channels. It also applies when an individual is officially representing Super91 in public spaces.

---

## Reporting & Escalation

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the organization leadership at:

* **Email:** [support@super91.com](mailto:support@super91.com)
* **Confidential Contact:** Organization Administrators (`@prints24/leads`)

All reports will be reviewed promptly and investigated impartially. All leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.
